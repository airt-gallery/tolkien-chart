# Sources for the Tolkien who's-who chart

Checked Sun 22 Nov 2026. Facts and names only; all wording on the chart is our own.

Primary (the published books, by J.R.R. Tolkien; the last three edited by Christopher Tolkien):
- *The Hobbit* (1937)
- *The Lord of the Rings* (1954–55), including the Prologue and Appendices A, B and F
- *The Silmarillion* (1977): Ainulindalë, Valaquenta, Quenta Silmarillion, Akallabêth, Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age
- *Unfinished Tales* (1980): "The Istari"
- *The History of Middle-earth* (1983–96), especially *The War of the Jewels* (the Quendi and Eldar essay: Imin, Tata and Enel) and *The Peoples of Middle-earth* (the Dwarf houses and Durin's waking at Gundabad)

Flagged as unsure or left open by Tolkien (shown on the chart, note 16):
- Where Orcs came from (the chart gives the best-known account: bred from captured Elves).
- Whether the Great Eagles are Maiar in eagle form or living birds.
- What the Huorns are; Tom Bombadil; the origins of Ungoliant and the Watcher in the Water.
- Gil-galad's father (Fingon in *The Silmarillion*; Orodreth's line in later notes), so the chart names no father.
- The 144 first Elves and the names Imin, Tata and Enel come from a late story, not *The Silmarillion*; the chart says "a later story".
- [CHECK] Dates follow the Tale of Years in Appendix B; "about 590 years" for the First Age is the usual reckoning, not a figure Tolkien gave in one place.
