# Tolkien who's-who chart

A free, printable who's who of J.R.R. Tolkien's world: 10 pages, A4 landscape, colour-coded by how each kind of being came to be. From Eru Ilúvatar and the Valar down to Elves, Men, Hobbits, Dwarves, Ents, Eagles and the servants of the Dark, with numbered notes at the back.

Built by MSTR BLDR on **AiRT Official**. An unofficial fan reference, written fresh in our own words.

## Pages

1. The whole family: everything on one page
2. The Powers: the fourteen Valar in their households, and their Maiar
3. The Elves and the Great Parting: Quendi, Eldar, Vanyar, Noldor, Teleri and the rest
4. Men, Hobbits and the kingdoms of Men
5. Lúthien's line: the Half-elven family tree, from Thingol and Melian to Aragorn and Arwen
6. Dwarves, Ents and Eagles: peoples begun by the Valar
7. The Dark and its servants
8. to 10. Notes 1 to 16, other names, a timeline of the Ages, how each kind came to be, places, where to read more

## How to use

**View it free:** https://airt-gallery.github.io/tolkien-chart/

**Download the PDF:** [tolkien-whos-who-chart.pdf](https://airt-gallery.github.io/tolkien-chart/tolkien-whos-who-chart.pdf)

Or download the files from this repo and print the PDF at 100% on A4 landscape, in colour. You can also open `index.html` in a modern browser and print from there.

## Files

- `tolkien-whos-who-chart.pdf`: the chart. Print at 100% on A4 landscape, in colour.
- `tolkien-whos-who-chart.html`: the source. Chrome lays out the boxes, draws the connecting lines, checks nothing spills off a page, and prints the PDF. The finished PDF has no scripts, links or form fields; fonts are embedded.

## Checks

- Every page rendered and looked at; an automatic check finds any box that spills past the page edge, into the footer, or is clipped (0 found).
- Names, dates and family links checked against the published books and Tolkien Gateway (see `SOURCES.md`). Points Tolkien left open or changed are flagged in note 16.
- Wording checked against our reference so the text is our own: no passages copied from the books or elsewhere.

## The ASTARR prompt used

PROBLEM: I can never keep track of who's who in Tolkien's world: Valar, Maiar, Elves, Men, Dwarves and the rest.

ASTARR PROMPT

ACT AS: A Tolkien scholar and information designer who makes clear, printable charts.

SPECIFIC TOPIC: A who's-who chart of Tolkien's world, from Eru Ilúvatar down to Elves, Men, Hobbits, Dwarves, Ents, Eagles and the servants of the Dark, for fans and readers of the books. Write it all in your own words: no quotes from the books, and no film images or official artwork.

ACTIONS: Research who made or fathered each kind of being, using the published books, and flag anything Tolkien left unclear or changed his mind on. Sort them into family-tree pages, colour-coded by how each kind came to be. Give the Valar, the Elves, Men, the Half-elven line, the Dwarves, Ents and Eagles, and the Dark their own pages. Add numbered notes that explain each part. Check every name, date and family link twice.

RESULT&RULES: I want a printable A4 landscape PDF of about 10 pages, with connected boxes, a colour key on every page and the notes at the back. Easy to read, family-friendly, and marked as an unofficial fan reference.

ASTARR = Act As, Specific Topic, Actions, Result, Rules.
